## Reading (descriptive, for the next step)
- The channel carries the localisation claim in this scene: without it (noC) the executor fails even the trained pairing K1 (0/60) while with it N1+N2 is .79-.82; frozen pi0.5 gets 0 on N1+N2 and 1.0 on K1+K2.  The model-side fix costs K1+K2 (-.26 to -.30 vs I), mainly K2 (.48-.55), and K2 is no better than the kitchen checkpoint's (.533), so it is not the new training data.
- W5 adds nothing measurable to RAA-sL-S2 here (+.025 N1+N2, -.042 K1+K2), as in the probe; the scene-2 mu table is within 1.2 cm of zero.
- The resolver analogue is trivially engaged (noR picks a plate by size: .50 wrong) and its removal costs .43; the pose-memory analogue is barely engaged (plates are rarely hidden in the agentview) and its removal costs nothing measurable; both are constructed analogues, not atlas cells.
- Against rgp (SR 68: .708 here .708 on new states) the privileged-channel arms are ahead on N1+N2 (+.108, lower +.017) and behind on K1+K2 (.70-.74 vs .99).  The comparison is not like for like: the channel and role views are privileged (simulator masks and positions), rgp's are OWLv2 + depth.
