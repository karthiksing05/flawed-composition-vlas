## What it says (consistent with, not established)
- Frozen pi0.5 does not release the carried bowl at the trained plate position when the plate moves: at far-out displacements (.14-.29 m) it releases within a median 2.0 cm of the actual plate, 0 of 40 trained releases, success .975.  The
  trained-coordinate account, read on the target, is not what I does; rgp and its release error are the same as I's.  This agrees with SR 70 (I .967 on a displaced, uncovered plate).
- R2, retrained on the 8 known tasks from role-painted views, shows the claimed behaviour on the target: 31 of 40 far-out episodes end the carry at the trained plate position (median 20 cm from the actual plate, though the painted plate is
  in view), and 4 of 40 do so at the near-out bin.  This is consistent with a retrained policy that learned the plate's coordinates (the 8 tasks put the plate in a 2 cm box) rather than the plate.  The test does not separate that
  from the role painting being ignored, and it has one retrained checkpoint, one seed.
- RAA (R2's views + the relative-position channel) does not release at the trained spot (1 of 40) but also does not reach the moved plate: .10 success, release error 6.0 cm, carry only .675.  The channel text carries offsets outside its
  training range at far-out distances; this note does not test that explanation.  Where the target is moved, RAA - I is -.875: M2's premise (RAA follows the moved target better than I) fails on these states.
- Object moved (descriptive): at far-out distances I, R2 and RAA mostly do not grasp the bowl (carry .45 / .12 / .57 on K1, .68 / .15 / .50 on K2); success .375 / 0 / .125 on K1 and .400 / .075 / .050 on K2; rgp, which reads the pose, keeps .85-.93.
  So an object-position cue is a better candidate than a target-position cue for I's failure here; the test does not say where the arm goes when it fails to grasp.

## Limits
- One scene, two tasks, one seed per state (client seed 7, server seed 13), 40 states per cell, one checkpoint per retrained arm.
- The in-range share is 0 by rule, so M1 only asks whether the far-out share is above 0; that M1 is NOT SUPPORTED for I is the far-out share being exactly 0 of 40.
- The reach attribution (the eef xy at the first close command within 10 cm of the bowl's start height) is descriptive and confounded by the approach path: I succeeds in 40 of 40 O1_near episodes yet 25% of them count as "trained attempts"
  (R2 .23-.73, RAA .23-.35, rgp 0-.28 across the object-moved cells), so these shares are not read as evidence for a trained-position grasp.
- The oracle track (simulator positions) is the read's instrument only; rgp, RAA and R2 use privileged depth, calibration or masks as in SR 52 / 73.
- R2's finding is descriptive (R2 - I trained share on T_far +.775 [+.625, +.900]); it is not a registered contrast and R2's K1_in success is below the .70 floor.
