## SR 82 diagnostic (exploratory, NOT registered): why RAA-sL full collapses on F6_b (rgc tid 8)

Read-only on results/flawed/wasl (chan logs, *_wa.json, *_track.npz; experiments/sr82_diag.py, cpu jobs 4807957-70, 4807967, 4807970 and a final one) plus two small GPU replays outside results/ (experiments/sr82_diag_serve.py = wa_s.py serve with a per-query log of servo vs expert; jobs 4807975 F6_b full + noM, 4808052 F6_a full; smoke states k400-403, 4 episodes each; logs in .claude_tmp/pp/sr82_replay*). No registered number changed.

**Hypotheses refuted.**
- Frozen relative offset: not present. wa_s.py recomputes rel every query from the absolute held pose (line 189 `rel = r[role] - ee`, the client sends `pos[t_i]` each query, line 520), minus the object offset fixed at the hold onset (lines 507-508, 197: raa.py's raao/raac rule). The channel error vs simulator truth for the held plate is small: 523 full-arm target queries, all src `occl`, none omitted, error median 1.31 cm, p90 2.24 (F6_a: 1.12 cm). Frame and units are right (servo 0.57 at rel 20 cm = K 2.85 x 0.2 m; role id 2 on every target query; m = 1).
- Wrong entity, wrong role, `on` flag, cm/m scaling: none (roles (bowl, plate_1), prompt unchanged, state in metres).
- wa.py (SR 79/80) has the same recompute (line 178, no offset since the text channel never had one); the flaw does not exist there, so SR 79/80 D2 is not affected by this.

**What the logs show (F6_b tid 8, 60 states).**
- Timing is the same as F6_a: first lift step 67 (full) vs 68.5 (F6_a), last target query t 85 vs 80, about 8 target queries; noM carries 14 queries and releases at step 124.
- Position is not: last target-role query before the episode leaves the target phase has |rel xy| median 5.4 cm in full F6_b (7% under 3 cm), 1.4 cm in full F6_a (78% under 3 cm); noM F6_b successes 2.2 cm. The bowl is released 3-7 cm from the plate (full: 16 of 52 releases at 3-5 cm, 26 at 5-7, 10 at over 7, none under 3, success 0 of 52); noM releases under 3 cm in 28 of 60 episodes with success .75 there, .08 at 3-5 cm.  So F6_b success needs a release within about 3 cm, and full never gets there. After the early release the object-role channel carries no servo and the episodes loop (8 gripper toggles median) to the 300-step cap.
- Servo vs expert (replay, raw action units, mean of the first 5 rows; expert = action minus servo): in the second half of the target phase the expert xy is strongly negative while the target is still ahead, F6_b full expert x -0.415 / -0.064 / -0.378 against servo x +0.089 / +0.274 / +0.108 (cos servo-expert -0.92 / -0.74 / -0.64; rel x +3-4 cm; net action x -0.33 / +0.21 / -0.27). F6_a full: expert x -0.048 / -0.045 / +0.015, rel reaches 0-1 cm. The proportional servo is 0.09 at 3.5 cm and cannot overcome the expert's -0.4, so rel xy stalls at 3-6 cm and the expert's descend-and-release then fires there.

**Cause (supported, one step short of proven).** The channel is right; the servo is a weak proportional term near the goal and relies on the flow expert to bring the last 3-6 cm. The expert was trained (residual = action - servo) on scenes in which the plate is visible, and in F6_b the opaque cover (radius 11.35 cm against the plate's 6.85, f6o_tasks.py COVER_MARGIN, non-colliding, rendered in both cameras) hides the plate, so the expert's xy braking and release fire 3-6 cm short of the target and the servo cannot pull the bowl in. The two cells share states, channel quality (1.1 vs 1.3 cm), timing and servo; they differ in the rendered cover, and the expert xy output differs accordingly. Without the servo (noM) the expert takes 14 queries and ends 2-3 cm closer on its own (success .383), and with the servo it arrives at about the same time but short. I did not run a cover-visible-but-hidden-from-channel ablation, so "the expert reads the cover as the plate" is inferred from the F6_a / F6_b contrast, not isolated.
Small replays: 3 of 4 episodes per cell have target queries in the log (the segmenter assigns the warmup query its own segment); noM replay target rows are zero-state by design.

**Concrete locus.** raas_model.py:58-59 (servo = clip(K*rel, max_step)*m: gain K = 2.85 per m, nothing that scales up near the goal) together with the Pi0S training target (action - servo): the expert is not trained to complete the last centimetres for a covered target. Not a client bug: wa_s.py:189-204 behave as designed.
