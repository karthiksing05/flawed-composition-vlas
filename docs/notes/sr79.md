Mechanism (server per-query log and client records). Resolver bound the correct referent 1.0 on all four templates in full and noM. noR's largest-component rule found it in .85 (relL), .15 (relR), 1.0 (pos), 0.0 (neg) of the episodes where it applied. Object-role channel error (median, cm): full relL 1.15, relR 1.02, pos .20, neg .73; noR relL 2.5, relR 19.2, pos 6.1, neg 14.3. noM omission share of queries: object role relL .53, relR .40, pos .07, neg .24 (F6_b: .22); target role F6_b .998, relL .38, relR .30. noMe withholds almost nothing on F6_b (object .01, target 0), as the smoke found: the plate's hold in F6_b is the occlusion rule's (target-role src stale share .994 in full).

**Reading.**
- R matters on F7 (D1): the gain is on the negation task (+.425) and relR (+.300), and consistent with the referent binding: noR binds the wrong bowl or averages (object-channel error 14-19 cm on neg / relR) and succeeds only where the unbound default happens to work (neg .55 = frozen I .55). pos is 0 in every RAA arm, relL is not separated.
- M does not show on F6_b (D2): full .050, noM 0, and full is at the executor's floor (F6_a, the unmoved-plate control, is .083 while frozen I gets .967 there). A removal test on a cell the executor fails cannot show M is needed; the result is consistent with RAA being unable to place at all on the displaced plate rather than with M being unnecessary.
- The cross-term D3b is not null: noM lowers F7 by .075 [.006, .144], driven by relR (-.200) and relL; consistent with the occlusion hold of M being used on F7 object-role queries (a quarter to a half of them omitted in noM), i.e. M is not F6-specific. noMe = full on F7 (0, exact), so the part of M that matters on F7 is the occlusion rule's hold, not the empty-mask hold.
- DK: R's removal costs nothing on K (replicate; the two arms differ by .008 from rollout noise).
- Against SR 70 references: RAA full is far below rc (frozen pi0.5 + F7R + slot + ReGuide) on F7 and F6_b, and equals frozen I on the F7 pool; RAA's executor, not the WHERE modules, sets the ceiling on these cells.
