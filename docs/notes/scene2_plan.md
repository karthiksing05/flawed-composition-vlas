## 4. Risks
- Data scale and mixture: 100 scene-2 cycles from one scene against 300 kitchen episodes; K1/K2 fit may be below .70 (then the arm is UNTESTABLE, as GR00T in SR 50).  Fallback: oversample scene-2 cycles 2x or train longer; changes the recipe, must be stated.
- Leakage: pi05_libero (the init) trained on the scene-2 compound; N1 / N2 are new pairings of seen parts, as in scene 1.  The compound's second-cycle start differs from a single-step task (the first mug may already sit on a plate): K1 / K2 cycles are not exactly the K1 / K2 test starts.
- Privileged inputs: sim masks and depth / calibration for the channel (as SR 50, 66, ReGuide-perc depth); an OWLv2 variant would add perception noise and the 5.65 cm held-mug error that limits rgp on N1.  State which is primary before the row.
- Servo / mu table constants fitted on kitchen (K 2.851, M .9375; rack-style release points) may not suit mug-on-plate carry (0.45 m carry, the mug's handle); refit from the 100 cycles only.  A W5 release-point table from 50 demos per plate has spread; the radius R = 3 cm is hand-set.
- Only two new pairings: the task-level bootstrap is not available; state-only bootstrap, wide intervals (N1+N2 n = 120 episodes).  Multiplicity uncorrected (about 8 arms).
- The resolver and pose-memory analogues are constructed; D1' risks being trivially satisfied (the position name is the only cue) and D2' may be empty because the plate is rarely occluded in the agentview.  Report as analogues, never as atlas cells.
- Step 0 may succeed spuriously (an N2 success from the channel alone could mask that K fell); read K floor first.
- Harness bugs: the kitchen client assumes kitchen entities (fa1_common.ENT, sc_pu calib); a bit-identity check against the kitchen run on one kitchen cell after the copy is required.  Never edit wa_s.py / wa_sw5.py / backbone_w.py modes 0-7 (add only).
- Compute: tail-lab 4-GPU cap; the rollout set is about 30 jobs, launch in waves; the SR 82 chains lost about 4 h to node waits.
