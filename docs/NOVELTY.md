# Novelty: where this work overlaps prior work and where it differs (2026-11-14)

Sources: `reports/novelty_check_failure_atlas.md` (arXiv abstracts for all ids; full texts for ATOM-Bench, Harness VLA, VLA-Corrector, Failing Forward), `reports/CoRL 2026 VLA literature review.md` (abstracts and summarising-tool reads), and abstract pages re-fetched on 2026-11-14 for ReGuide, LiLo-VLA, AC-VLA, CRAFT and ACT-VLA. Our numbers come from `docs/ARCH_FAILURES.md` and `docs/ARCHITECTURE_PROPOSAL.md` (SR = SCAFFOLD_RESULTS). Limits of the check: most rows rest on abstracts; LIBERO-Gen has no full text; the lit review covers about 300 of 687 CoRL papers; none of the five target papers' full texts was re-read here. Where a row says "abstract only", the paper may do more than stated.

Our setting for every number below: one LIBERO-style scene, one seed per rung, 20-60 fresh states per confirmation, intervals are 95%. Priors report other benchmarks, so success rates do not compare across rows.

## 1. Prior work table

"Frozen at test time" asks whether the paper edits a frozen, unmodified VLA at inference with no training.

## 2. Our claims, ranked by novelty

Ranked from most to least novel. "Nearest prior" is the closest published result; the verdict says how much of the claim survives it.

1. **Silhouettes beat whole-wrist grey at a matched prompt on frozen pi0.5.** Evidence: TV+P .817 vs W+P .375, +.442 [.208, .675] on 20 fresh states; W+P costs .23 on trained tasks; W also breaks the hold detector (multiple firings in 65% of episodes) (SR §48). Nearest prior: AC-VLA (masks the wrist view while the gripper is closed; trained; no identity-vs-geometry ablation) and LiLo-VLA (erases non-target objects). Verdict: the matched-prompt separation is new. It holds only against W, our proxy for AC-VLA, only on pi0.5, in one scene. On GR00T the separation fails (+.236, lower bound misses 0 by .017), and the trained comparison R2 - R2w is not separated.

2. **Cross-chunk F1 break across five frozen VLAs, with per-stage excess over known partners, replicated on fresh states.** Evidence: cross-chunk excess +.45 pi0.5, +.50 GR00T, +.225 pi0, +.379 X-VLA fresh; SmolVLA fails within-chunk (+.862); the new layout reads NEITHER on all five. Nearest prior: CRAFT Table A.4 (38.4% of pi0.5 held-out rollouts take the grasped object to its trained partner; GR00T 39.3%), and Failing Forward Table S9 (first-failure labelling, one task). Verdict: the behaviour is prior. The stage assignment, the excess-over-partner estimator and the five-model, fresh-state replication are new. "Hand-off" as a stage has no prior. The atlas rests on one scene and 20 fresh states.

3. **F7: relational language is ignored, the relational choice equals the ambiguous choice on 92-100% of states, and a resolver plus role view fixes 3 of 4 cells.** Nearest prior: LIBERO-PRO (negation and nonsense instructions leave outputs unchanged), LIBERO-CF, LIBERO-Plus (language perturbations barely matter), Harness VLA (binding moved out of the VLA). Verdict: "VLAs ignore language" is prior. The unique-name control, the choice-equals-ambiguous-choice statistic, and the resolver with a role view on frozen models are new but narrow. The resolver covers fixed templates on 2 relation families; on pi0, X-VLA and SmolVLA it fixes 0 of 6 cells, capped by each model's named-referent ceiling. Two of the six non-fixed cells in the extension have no power.

4. **Memory slot on frozen VLAs (F6).** Evidence: plate seen once, then covered; M - b +.26 [+.15, +.35] on pi0.5 (.58 to .84), +.14 [+.04, +.24] on GR00T (.14 to .28), 120 new states. Nearest prior: MEM, pi0.7 history, a memory-augmented world model, RoboTTT, all of which add memory by training. Verdict: repainting a remembered target into both cameras of an unmodified VLA is new as far as we found. It rests on one occluder task, GR00T sits at the rule's boundary, M recovers 24% of the cost on GR00T, the plate leaks back into view on 45-49% of later queries under M vs 34-43% under b, and the occluder rule changed twice before any rollout.

5. **F6 memory-absent pattern with the occluder.** Evidence: covering costs .38 (pi0.5) and .60 (GR00T) (d - b); b - c is +.05 [-.05, +.16] and +.04 [-.03, +.12]. Nearest prior: none measured on frozen VLAs; the lit review lists memory as a design trend. Verdict: the registered verdict is PARTIAL on both models, because the equivalence bound misses .10 by .058 and .017. The cost of covering is established. "Success stays at the never-seen control" is not.

6. **Chunk length, release timing and the partner-count law ruled out.** Nearest prior: no direct test; Wang et al. state the law on a small policy. Verdict: new, and all three are weak negatives. Chunking rules out only large gains (intervals allow +.15 on pi0.5 and +.23 on GR00T). Release timing reads NOT TIMING with pi0.5's lower bound at exactly 0, and the author's prediction narrowly missed. The partner-count law is REVERSED on GR00T and UNCLEAR-to-HOLDS on pi0.5 (+.167 [0, .333] at 2 per query; +.317 [.167, .467] at 10), with a shape confound open.

7. **New layouts fail every system, including an exact-position planner, with no pairing-specific excess.** Nearest prior: Brittle Grounding jitter results, policies only. Verdict: new as a planner-control finding, but it is a failure of criterion 1 (the reference fails too), so it is a limit on the task, not on the VLA.

8. **Role views and role phrases on frozen pi0.5 (.450 to .817, +.367 [.092, .634]; no trained-task cost).** Nearest prior: ReGuide (frozen pi0.5 39.0 to 94.5), LiLo-VLA and AC-VLA masking, VP-VLA phrases. Verdict: a frozen-model test-time fix of this kind has prior art. We claim confirmation, not priority. On GR00T views alone gain +.186 [+.033, +.361] and phrases hurt.

9. **Hand-over variant.** Nearest prior: ReGuide, Harness VLA, LiLo-VLA. Verdict: no novelty claim (GR00T .17 to .84; pi0.5 .50 to .85 is NOT SHOWN, interval [+.017, +.675]).

## 3. Claims to drop or soften

- **Drop "the hand-over variant is new".** ReGuide, Harness VLA and LiLo-VLA hand control between a VLA and a non-learned component. Say it reproduces that idea with learned perception.
- **Drop any priority claim for a frozen-VLA test-time front end.** ReGuide is the training-free baseline and reaches 94.5 on its benchmark. Our .817 is on 20 fresh states in one scene. On our own states (SR §52) a reimplemented ReGuide reaches .883 (perception) and .933 (oracle) against our .817, NO CLEAR DIFFERENCE, ahead on N4/N6 because it takes the carry from the policy.
- **Soften "silhouettes beat AC-VLA".** Say "silhouettes beat a whole-wrist grey proxy at a matched prompt on frozen pi0.5". AC-VLA is a training framework; W is not its method; the retrained comparison and the GR00T comparison are not separated; one abstract-versus-Sec. 3.3 inconsistency (closed-gripper vs place) in AC-VLA is unresolved.
- **Soften "first stage-level atlas".** Failing Forward Table S9 labels each failed episode by its first failure. Claim the excess over known partners, the multi-model held-out-pairing setting and the "hand-off" stage.
- **Soften "memory-absent".** Write "covering the plate after one view costs .38 and .60; success within .05 of the never-seen control, but equivalence is not shown (PARTIAL)".
- **Soften "memory restored" on GR00T.** M's lower bound equals b's upper bound; M recovers 24% of the cost; late leakage into view may carry part of the gain. State the single-occluder scope.
- **Soften "chunk length ruled out" and "release timing ruled out".** Say "no large effect". The intervals leave modest effects open, and pi0.5's timing result sits at the rule's edge.
- **Soften "partner-count law refuted".** Say "not reproduced on two frozen VLAs", with the shape confound and the pi0.5 UNCLEAR/HOLDS split stated. Wang et al. test a different policy class.
- **Soften "F7 fixed".** Fixed on 3 of 4 cells for pi0.5 and GR00T only, resolver restricted to fixed templates, 0 of 6 on pi0, X-VLA, SmolVLA. Some cells are NO POWER. The resolver is exact (240/240) on a symbolic scene description, so this is a routing fix, not a language-understanding result.
- **Soften F2.** Architecture-rooted on pi0 and SmolVLA only; frozen pi0.5 and GR00T grasp right at .95-.99. Our retrain fix (R0 .408 to R1 .925) carries no CI in SR §37.1, and role phrases alone read NO CLEAR DIFFERENCE.
- **Soften any mechanism sentence on F1.** Our records test consequences, not the attention path. Grant et al. hold the mechanistic evidence.
- **State the scope everywhere.** One scene, one seed per rung, 20-60 fresh states, LIBERO-Goal descendant. The TTIC/UChicago benchmark audit (2606.04233) shows LIBERO is solvable without language, so held-out-pairing claims need per-instance reporting, which our intervals already provide.
- **F3 and the new layout are not VLA claims.** The reference planner fails the same states; report as a limit.
