# CoRL 2026 overview, awards, program and trends

Snapshot date: 2026-11-10 (the conference is in progress, not finished; see Gaps). Search was limited to a handful of calls.

## Dates, venue, organizers, submission statistics

### Takeaway
CoRL 2026 is in Austin, Texas, US. Official pages disagree on the exact day layout (see below). 2,094 active submissions and 687 accepted papers (about 32.8 to 33 percent), roughly triple the 2025 submission count per a program chair.

### Cited Findings
- Location: Austin, Texas, US. Venues: JW Marriott Austin (poster sessions, exhibits), Hilton Austin (presentations, keynotes on Days 1 and 2), Bass Hall (reception and Day 3 keynote) — [corl.org](https://www.corl.org)
- Dates, source A: corl.org home page text reads main conference Nov 9-11, workshops Nov 12 — [corl.org](https://www.corl.org). The page title says "November 9-12, 2026" — [2026.corl.org](https://2026.corl.org/)
- Dates, source B (conflicting): the official call for workshops says workshops are held Nov 9 at the JW Marriott Austin, the day before the main conference (Nov 10-12), all half-day — [call for workshops](https://2026.corl.org/contributions/call-for-workshops). RuntimeWire also gives workshops Nov 9, main Nov 10-12 — [RuntimeWire](https://runtimewire.com/article/corl-2026-austin-robot-learning). The workshop call says the schedule is preliminary; the corl.org home page appears to be the later source, but unverified which is final. UT Austin events page lists Nov 9 start — [UT](https://ai.utexas.edu/events/2026-11-09/2026-conference-robot-learning)
- General chairs: Yuke Zhu (UT Austin, NVIDIA), Peter Stone (UT Austin, Sony AI). Program chairs: Ani Majumdar (Princeton), Dieter Fox (UW, AI2), Georgia Chalvatzaki (TU Darmstadt). Workshop chairs: Danfei Xu (Georgia Tech), Rudolf Lioutikov (KIT). Demo chair: Guanya Shi (CMU). OpenReview chair: Carlo Sferrazza (UT Austin). Others: Local Joydeep Biswas, Roberto Martin-Martin, April Cafaro; Publications Yoonchang Sung; Virtual Tapomayukh Bhattacharjee; Finance David Fridovich-Keil; Inclusion Chuchu Fan; Web Ian Abraham; Publicity Dinesh Jayaraman; Sponsorship Sandeep Chinchali, Dhruv Shah, Neel Bhatt — [organizing committee](https://2026.corl.org/organizing-committee)
- 2,094 active submissions, 687 accepted, about 33 percent (the @corl_conf account gives 32.8 percent); Chalvatzaki said submissions were nearly three times last year's — [Digg summary of X posts](https://digg.com/ai/ozl3pl3c); originals: [Chalvatzaki](https://x.com/GeorgiaChal/status/2095935964786954746), [@corl_conf](https://x.com/corl_conf/status/2095971635593404733), [Majumdar](https://x.com/Majumdar_Ani/status/2095977513587560672) (I did not open the X posts themselves; the numbers come via Digg)
- Timeline: submission deadline May 28, 2026 AoE; reviewing Jun 15 - Jul 13; notification Sep 4; camera-ready Oct 12 — [instruction for authors](https://2026.corl.org/contributions/instruction-for-authors) (via search snippet)
- Format: all accepted papers are posters; selected papers get oral spotlights; proceedings in PMLR — [instruction for authors](https://2026.corl.org/contributions/instruction-for-authors) (via search snippet). Authors must supply a reviewer; reviews/rebuttals of accepted papers are made public — [instruction for reviewers](https://www.corl.org/contributions/instruction-for-reviewers)

## Keynotes

### Takeaway
Four named people across three sessions, all on embodied AI, deployment and world models.

### Cited Findings
- Wolfram Burgard (Univ. of Technology Nuremberg), "Challenges to Realize Embodied AI": handling uncertainty, combining probabilistic and deep-learning methods, foundation models for changing environments.
- Russ Tedrake (MIT; co-founder/CEO Walden Robotics), "Learning in the factories": making large manipulation models useful in real applications, how performance scales with deployment, based on a live production deployment.
- Fei-Fei Li (Stanford; World Labs), "Atlas: A Unified World Model for Generation and Reconstruction": autoregressive frame generation unifying pixel generation and 3D reconstruction; second half a fireside chat with Lisa Su (Chair/CEO, AMD).
- All from [keynotes page](https://2026.corl.org/program/keynotes). Dates/times of each not listed. Early career keynotes: call posted 06/24 ([announcements](https://2026.corl.org/announcements)); selected speakers not found.
