## Verdict per component
1. Per-role queries built only from that role's phrase tokens, with no cross-role attention: **PARTIAL.** RoboGround already has separate object and placement queries, but its masks come from a VLM that reads the whole instruction. Building each query from that role's phrase tokens alone, with no cross-role attention, is new.
2. Role query cross-attends to visual tokens to produce a soft mask and slot feature, with two-hop "A of B": **PARTIAL.** Mask-guided role queries (RoboGround) and relational two-stage grounding (INGRESS, MAttNet) both exist. Learned two-hop resolution inside a VLA's slot query was not found.
3. Position-free matching keys, with position only through a relative-geometry channel: **PARTIAL.** The principle is in ISA (slot frames), POCR (what/where), Act3D (relative attention), and role/filler and NoPE work. Applying it to VLA grounding keys was not found.
4. Action expert sees the scene only through role slots + object-frame pose + proprio: **PARTIAL.** A hard slot bottleneck exists in Pelican-VLA 0.5 (generic slots), and pose-bearing object records appear in 2607.18016. A role-indexed bottleneck with object-frame pose was not found.
5. V-REx/IRM across scene layouts: **NOVEL in a VLA (in what I searched)**, but IRM in IL is prior art (2308.09189, 2311.01489), so this is an application rather than a new method.
6. MCD splits + theory-predicted per-component ablations: **NOVEL as evaluation methodology for VLAs** (no MCD in robotics found). Graded generalization levels exist (VIMA L1-L4, ATOM-Bench, RoboHiMan).
**Combination: NOVEL.** No paper combines role-isolated language-built queries + bottleneck + position-free keys + invariance + MCD-tested binding predictions. Each piece has a precedent, so the contribution is the theory-driven assembly plus falsifiable ablations, not any single module.

## Strongest reviewer objection
"This is RoboGround (object + placement queries over masks) plus Pelican/SlotVLA-style slot bottlenecks, with a known invariance loss bolted on. The role parse is hand-specified: you need the instruction split into role phrases, which is a supervision or oracle signal LIBERO templates hand you for free and that breaks on free-form language. Gains on MCD splits may come from the oracle role parse and from removing distractor access, not from binding." The defence must include: (a) a learned or noisy role parser, plus a free-form paraphrase test; (b) baselines matched to RoboGround (two masked query sets) and Pelican (generic K-slot bottleneck) at equal token budget; (c) each predicted ablation failing in the predicted direction on the predicted split. Secondary objection: V-REx is weak when environments barely differ (Pareto-IRM 2206.07766), and LIBERO layouts are few and similar.
