**Figure 1 (fig_atlas). The measured break of each failure, by model.** Each panel shows one failure on the five frozen VLAs; rows are models, and a blank row means no read. F1 and F2 show the excess failure E over known partners, with 95% intervals (F1: scene 1 filled, scene 2 open; GR00T sits below its testability floor in scene 2). F6 shows the cost of covering the plate after one view, d - b, with 95% intervals; pi0 is not testable. Frozen pi0.5 is grey in every figure.

**Figure 3 (fig_arms). Success of every trained variant, per task.** Top: the six new pairings N1-N6 and their pool. Bottom: the six trained tasks K1-K6 and their pool. Each point is success over 60 states of one task (one seed per variant); bars are Wilson 95% intervals and treat states as independent, so the pooled intervals understate the task-level spread. R0 and R2 are plain retrains, RAA adds the role channel, RAA-o measures the target from the held object, RAA-c feeds the offset as a state token, RAA-s adds a servo term, and RAA-L and RAA-sL (open markers) start from the LIBERO-fine-tuned pi0.5. ReGuide with perceived poses (black) is the external reference. RAA-s is the re-serve with the servo term.

**Figure 4 (fig_removal). Removing one module returns one failure.** D1: success on the relational (F7) cells for the full policy and for the policy without the referent resolver R. D2: success on the hidden-plate cell (F6, condition b) for the full policy and for the policy without the pose memory M. Left pair of bars: RAA-L (SR 80). Right pair: RAA-sL (SR 82). Error bars are 95% intervals from the registered reads, and the grey line and band give frozen pi0.5 on the same states. Removing R lowers F7 success in both policies. Removing M does not lower F6 success in RAA-L beyond the interval, and it raises F6 success in RAA-sL, where the full policy scores .017 on that cell. Section 6.3 traces that reversal to the flow expert's xy near a covered target (SR 82 diagnostic), and the test-time variant that gives the module the xy of the carry restores F6 (SR 84) and is not drawn here.
