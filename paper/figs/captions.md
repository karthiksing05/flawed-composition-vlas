# Figure captions (draft)

Numbering follows docs/notes/fig_plan.md (new numbering, 2026-11-21): main Fig 1-6, appendix A1-A16. Old to new: fig_atlas (old Fig 1) = Fig 3; fig_arms (old Fig 3) = A11; fig_moved (old Fig 2) = A12; fig_removal (old Fig 4) is replaced by fig_removal2 = Fig 5 (the W5 where-module reads move to A14). Wording rule: "held-out" and "training" layouts and pairings, "fresh set N" only as the established name of the SR 87/90/91 state sets.

Frame rule (paper/figs/frames/selection.json, fixed before any frame was looked at). Every episode drawn in Fig 1, Fig 2 and A2-A9, A14, A16 is a development-state episode (n2_states with n2_branches_main.json, rg_comp main states, mt states). Per entry, over its first 10 trials in branches-file order, a trial qualifies iff the failing arm fails with the failure's own signature and the comparator arm succeeds on the same trial of the same fresh run. The shown episode is the lowest-index qualifying trial, and A16 shows the next three qualifying trials. If no trial of the first 10 qualifies, 10 more trials are run and the same rule applies. The episodes are not a sample: they are chosen by outcome, and the registered rate in each caption is the cell's rate over all its states. The F6 signature was revised once, on 2026-11-21 after docs/notes/f6_diag.md and before the selection was recomputed (from "released more than 3 cm away" to "failed and the final bowl xy more than 3 cm from the plate centre", the harness predicate; the original version flagged 6 of 50 pi0.5 F6_b failures). Frames come from fresh rollouts that log their own outcome, so a re-run of a registered state can differ from its registered result.

**Figure 4 (fig_arch).
