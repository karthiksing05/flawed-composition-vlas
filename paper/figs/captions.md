**Figure 1 (fig_atlas). The measured break of each failure, by model.** Each panel shows one failure on the five frozen VLAs; rows are models, and a blank row means no read. F1 and F2 show the excess failure E over known partners, with 95% intervals (F1: scene 1 filled, scene 2 open; GR00T sits below its testability floor in scene 2). F1m shows the share of grasped N4 and N6 episodes that start the high carry (point, lower bound as a triangle, or range; the record has no intervals). F7 shows the share of states where the relational choice equals the ambiguous choice (SP filled, NG open; the bars give the 75-100% agreement range pooled over pi0, X-VLA and SmolVLA). F6 shows the cost of covering the plate after one view, d - b, with 95% intervals; pi0 is not testable. Frozen pi0.5 is grey in every figure.

**Figure 3 (fig_arms). Success of every trained variant, per task.** Top: the six new pairings N1-N6 and their pool. Bottom: the six trained tasks K1-K6 and their pool. Each point is success over 60 states of one task (one seed per variant). Bars are 95% percentile intervals from a cluster bootstrap over tasks and states (B = 2000, rng seed 0) on the per-episode rollouts: the pooled bar resamples the six tasks, then the 60 states within each drawn task, so it carries task-level spread; the per-task bars resample the 60 states of that task. R0 and R2 are plain retrains, RAA adds the role channel, RAA-o measures the target from the held object, RAA-c feeds the offset as a state token, RAA-s adds a servo term, and RAA-L and RAA-sL (open markers) start from the LIBERO-fine-tuned pi0.5. ReGuide with perceived poses (black) is the external reference. RAA-s is the re-serve with the servo term.

**Figure 4 (fig_removal). Removing one module returns one failure.** Top row, registered states: D1: success on the relational (F7) cells for the full policy and for the policy without the referent resolver R. D2: success on the hidden-plate cell (F6, condition b) for the full policy and for the policy without the pose memory M. Left pair of bars: RAA-L (SR 80). Right pair: RAA-sL (SR 82). Error bars are 95% intervals from the registered reads, and the grey line and band give frozen pi0.5 on the same states. Removing R lowers F7 success in both policies. Removing M does not lower F6 success in RAA-L beyond the interval, and it raises F6 success in RAA-sL, where the full policy scores .017 on that cell. Section 6.3 traces that reversal to the flow expert's xy near a covered target (SR 82 diagnostic), and the test-time variants that give the module the xy of the carry restore F6 (SR 84, SR 85) and are not drawn here. The SR 90 and SR 91 replications on fresh sets and the SR 89 scene-2 read appear in Table 2c and §6.2-6.3 and are not drawn. Bottom row, fresh states (SR 87, RAA-sL-W5, results/flawed/raasw5_wa_read_w5.json): full against noR on the pooled F7 cells (D1': +.483 [+.425, +.538]) and full against noM on F6 condition b (D2': +.217 [+.033, +.383]), with the registered 95% intervals and frozen pi0.5 on the same states; the gated primary is DISSOCIATION SHOWN. Hatched bars are the arms with the module removed, as in the legend.

**Figure 2 (fig_moved). A moved target: frozen pi0.5 follows the plate, a narrow retrain releases at the trained position.** Left: success of frozen pi0.5, the narrow retrain R2, RAA and ReGuide with perceived poses, on eight cells of 40 states each. K1 puts the bowl on the plate and K2 puts it on the stove. "Plate" cells move the plate, and "bowl" cells move the bowl, by a near or far distance (SR 81). Plate cells span 4.0-6.9 cm (near) and 14.1-28.9 cm (far), and bowl cells span 4.2-6.9 and 14.6-29.7 cm for K1 and 4.1-6.9 and 14.5-29.6 cm for K2 (results/flawed/mt_read.json). Right: the share of episodes whose release lies more than 3 cm closer to the trained plate position than to the actual plate, on K1 with the plate moved. The share is 0 by construction in range. Bars are 95% bootstrap intervals from the registered read.
