  rgt   rgp + tracking of the held mug during the attachment: OWLv2 re-detects the held mug every k control steps and its tracked
        centre replaces the rigid estimate (the hand-frame offset is re-anchored to the measurement), so the carry steers the tracked
        mug, not the assumed rigid offset, to the plate.  Rendered depth kept: rgt - rgp isolates problem (b).
  rgtp  rgt with rendered depth replaced by table-plane depth: each box's camera ray (bottom-centre for a mug on the table, centre
        for a plate) cut with the known table-plane height; the held mug's height from proprioception (hand height + the grasp
        offset).  Camera intrinsics and extrinsics stay (the remaining assumption).

What I expect mechanistically.
- If SR 68's account is right, rgp's carry ends with the real white mug a few cm off the estimate (the grasp slips / the mug swings
  in the hand), outside the right plate's release region; tracking should cut the release-time error and move N1 toward rgo.  The
  risks: (1) the held-mug box is partly occluded by the gripper, so the re-detected centre is biased or noisy (OWLv2 + inner-half
  depth median picks up finger pixels), and tracking could even hurt; (2) the N1 residual may not be the estimate at all but the
  policy after the hand-back (pi0.5 re-grasps / carries the mug back toward the left plate, SR 57-61's routing), which tracking
  does not touch.  rgo's .833 says the estimate matters, but rgo also uses simulator poses for the registration and the onset.
  I expect part of the gap to close, not all.
- rgtp: plane-cut localisation of a resting mug from the box bottom edge should be about as good as the depth median for the plates
  and mugs (1-2 cm); the held mug from a plane at hand height is rougher.  I expect a small cost, within the band on K1+K2 and
  probably within it on N1+N2.
