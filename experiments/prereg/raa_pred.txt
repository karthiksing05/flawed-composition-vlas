RAA-min (SR 73; docs/PLAN_PAPER.md 2b).  Written 2026-11-16, before the training jobs start and before any rollout of Rrel or RAA.  Known when written: SR 34, 37 (R0 .025 and R2 .425 on N1-N6 familiar, dev states 4-23; R2 grasps the right object .975 and places .436 given a right grasp; K1-K6 R0 .85, R2 .758), SR 43 (R2l .417), SR 52, 55, 65 (ReGuide on frozen pi0.5: rgp .929 on N1-N6), SR 70-72.

The test.  One added input, the active role entity's position minus the end effector's, in whole centimetres, with a role id, as a text suffix on the prompt of each routed frame (K1-K6; N1-N6 at test).  Rrel = R0's inputs + the channel.  RAA = R2's inputs (role phrases, the other movable objects painted) + the channel.  Same frames, recipe (frozen VLM, action expert, batch 32, 6,000 steps, lr 5e-5, 1,000 warmup, no EMA) and one seed as R0 and R2.  Test time: the channel comes from learned masks + rendered depth + calibration (sc_pu.Estimator), the pointer from the contact-induced lexicon (role_parse); the server paints R2's views from the learned masks (as R2l).  The prompt carries no other new information.

States: 60 familiar-layout states per task: the 20 dev states R0 / R2 / R2l were read on (harness trials 4-23, read from SR 37 / 43 unchanged) and 40 new states (k = 540-579, harness trials 24-63).  Tasks K1-K6 and N1-N6.  Every arm is read on all 60.

What would count against the plan's claim.  RAA - R2 with an upper bound under +.10 on both V1a and V1b while RAA's right-grasp rate stays at R2's: then a position channel in the prompt does not close the place gap at matched data, and the place failure is not an addressing failure alone.  Rrel - R0 near 0 and RAA - Rrel near RAA - R2: the views carry what gain there is and the channel adds nothing.
