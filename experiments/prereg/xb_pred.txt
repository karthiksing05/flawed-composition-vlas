XB (tag xb; docs/PLAN_GOAL3.md; docs/notes/bench_audit.md): cross-benchmark failure atlas on released checkpoints, evaluated frozen, plus a frozen failure-predictor test.
Held-out cell = an (object, receptacle) pair with zero co-occurrence in the data the checkpoint was trained on (audit files in results/bench/<name>/audit/).  Partner cell = a trained pair sharing the object or the receptacle with a held-out cell.
Cells are fixed by the rules below before any rollout; replacements happen only for policy-free reasons (scene fails to reset, expert planner fails), by the same rule for held-out and partner cells, logged.

Benchmarks, models, cells:
 xbM MESA (robosuite, Franka).  Models: albertwilcox mesa-pi0, mesa-pi05, mesa-pi0-fast, mesa-gr00t-n1.6 (all trained on mesa-70-lerobot).  Held-out: the 17 mesa-composite tasks clean against mesa-70 (cells__mesa-composite__vs__mesa-70-lerobot.json; excluded: carrot top-drawer, potato microwave, fish_garlic_pan_on).  Partners: every mesa-70 task sharing the held-out task's object or receptacle.  50 episodes per task, the suite's fixed init states 0-49.
 xbB BiMESA (bimanual YAM).  Models: the four bimesa-* checkpoints (bimesa-57-lerobot).  Held-out: the 8 fully clean bimesa-composite tasks; partners as in xbM.  50 episodes per task.
 xbC RoboCasa365 (MuJoCo, PandaOmron).  Models: pi0, pi0.5, GR00T N1.5 (official, pretrain_human300) and X-VLA (pepijn223 card: pretrain_human300_v4).  SmolVLA, GR00T N1.6 community and lerobot/pi05_robocasa are excluded (training data not auditable).  Families: the three pick-place families with most zero cells among {counter->microwave, counter->sink, counter->cabinet} (audit names).  Per family: 10 held-out cells drawn with np.random.default_rng(0) from zero-count (object category, family) cells whose object has >= 5 episodes in other families; 10 partner cells drawn with rng(1) from cells of the same family with count >= 3.  Object forced by obj_groups.  Placeability: a cell whose object fails to spawn in 3 resets is replaced by the next draw.  20 episodes per cell, seeds 0-19.
 xbT RoboTwin 2.0 (SAPIEN, aloha-agilex, demo_clean).  Models: X-VLA and pi0.5 official cotrain checkpoints, lerobot/smolvla_robotwin.  Tasks place_a2b_left, place_a2b_right.  Per task: 20 held-out instance pairs drawn with rng(0) from zero-count (A,B) pairs (A, B each >= 5 demos, different categories); 20 partner pairs drawn with rng(1) from pairs with count >= 2.  (A,B) forced by a load_actors patch (patch checked: 20 forced resets return the requested ids).  Eval seeds from 100000 upward, skipping seeds the expert planner fails for the forced pair (same rule for both cell types).  10 episodes per cell.

Measures: success (benchmark's own checker).  Excess failure E(model, benchmark) = mean failure over held-out cells minus mean failure over partner cells.
Bootstrap (every interval): resample cells with replacement within cell type, then episodes within cell; B = 2000, np.random.default_rng(0); 95% percentile interval.
Gate: partner-cell success < .30 for a (model, benchmark): UNTESTABLE there (floor), E reported descriptively only.
Failure classes (policy-free logs of object and receptacle poses): S success; G object never lifted 2 cm; W released on or in a non-instructed receptacle; O lifted, released on no receptacle; H still held at the end; X other.  Reported as shares, held-out vs partner.

Registered contrasts:
 x1 MESA: E > 0 (lower > 0) for at least 3 of the 4 testable mesa models.  Theory (P1 shortcut): objects have 1-4 training partners.
 x2 RoboTwin: E inside +-.10 (EQUIVALENT) for at least 2 of 3 testable models.  Theory: instances have 2-23 random partners, so the data broke the pair correlation and composition should hold.
 x3 RoboCasa: E > 0 for at least 2 of the testable models.
 x4 BiMESA: E > 0 for at least 3 of the 4 testable bimesa models.
 x5 Partner-count law: over all held-out cells of all benchmarks and testable models, Spearman rho between cell failure minus that model's partner-cell failure and log(min(object partner count, receptacle partner count)) is < 0 with the bootstrap interval (cells resampled) below 0.
Failure predictor (frozen before this file: v0 cue vote; v1 results/flawed/fp_dev/v1_frozen.json sha256 6bd3f414...; v2 f00ec7c1... where the probe exists):
 Features per held-out and partner cell, computed before any held-out rollout: pairing novelty and partner counts from the audit; trained-cue conflict and nearer-receptacle conflict from the reset state of each episode; language form from the instruction; base rate = the model's success on that benchmark's partner cells (partner rollouts run first).  Features a benchmark cannot supply take v1's stored training mean.
 Predictions are written to results/bench/xb/pred_<bench>.json (with file sha256 in the log) after partner rollouts and BEFORE any held-out rollout starts.
 p1: on held-out cells, AUC of v1 for cell failure (cell failure = success < .5) exceeds b2 (base rate only) with lower > 0 on at least 2 of the 3 benchmarks xbM, xbC, xbT (pooled over testable models).
 p2: v0 AUC > .5 (lower > .5) pooled over all benchmarks.
Holm over {x1, x2, x3, x5, p1} (one-sided bootstrap p; survives iff adjusted < .025; x2 uses the TOST p).
Budget: hard cap 300 GPU-h for all xb_* jobs.
Predictions:
 x1 SUPPORTED 55% (pooled E +.15).
 x2 EQUIVALENT 45%.
 x3 SUPPORTED 35% (low base rates, accidental zeros).
 x4 SUPPORTED 50%.
 x5 SUPPORTED 50%.
 p1 SUPPORTED 30%.

Kill: (k1) a model fails to load or reproduce its published in-distribution number within .15 on the partner cells (where one is published): excluded, reported; (k2) infrastructure failure: no read until every expected cell exists.
